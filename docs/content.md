# Content rules and sources

Every fact on the site has to be traceable to a source someone can open. This file lists those sources, the rules for
writing new text, and the figures that change over time with the date they were read.

## Writing rules

Every new or rewritten public text (site copy in `src/content/{en,es}`, case studies, the OhMyCV resumes and the READMEs
of the showcased repositories) goes through three passes before it is merged:

1. **Humanizer** (`~/.claude-personal/plugins/marketplaces/humanizer/SKILL.md`, v3.1.0). It removes the patterns of
   machine-written prose without adding or dropping facts. It never touches recommendation quotes or their
   translations, proper names, certificate titles, code identifiers or date ranges.
2. **HumanScope, the rules that fit factual text** (`anthropic-skills:humanscope`). The skill is written for fiction,
   and most of it (non-linear time, subplots, holding the key fact back) would hide or invent facts here. What applies:
    - No sentence states a lesson or a moral ("I learned that…"), and the narrator does not generalize.
    - Name the system, tool, team or institution instead of "several teams" or "organizations". Clients stay anonymous.
    - No stock phrases ("learning that never stops"), no sentence that repeats what the one before showed, and no em
      dash as punctuation.
    - Case studies keep what came from outside (a vendor change, a client decision), what each decision cost and what
      is still open, as long as it really happened.
3. **Fact-check at the Deep tier.** Claims the web can verify (institutions, issuers, course names, technology
   versions, live demos) are checked against at least two sources. Claims the web cannot see (private repositories, job
   titles) are checked against the primary source listed below. Every ❌ or ⚠️ is fixed before merging.

On top of that:

- No invented metrics. A figure appears only with a source and a date; otherwise the sentence ends at the action.
- First person, active voice, plain words. Colombian Spanish with *tú*; US English.
- Spanish headings capitalize only the first word and proper names ("Experiencia profesional"). Job titles and the names
  of degrees and courses keep the form used in the resume.
- Clients of TIVENOS and ADCOM are never named; `src/content/content.test.ts` fails if one appears.
- Quotes from recommendations are verbatim. Cut text is marked with `[…]`.
